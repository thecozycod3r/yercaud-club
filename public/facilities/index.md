# Yercaud Club facilities

The club has eight facilities: tennis, badminton, billiards, table tennis, gym, locker room, sports lounge, and dining.

## Tennis

Outdoor tennis court. Published member rate: ₹100 per day or ₹4,000 per annum. Member guests and affiliated members: ₹250 guest charge plus ₹250 per day, per person.

## Badminton

Indoor badminton court. Published member rate: ₹100 per day or ₹4,000 per annum. Member guests and affiliated members: ₹250 guest charge plus ₹250 per day, per person.

## Billiards

Subscribed members: ₹100 per month or ₹1,200 per annum. Non-subscribed members: ₹250 per person, per day. Member guests and affiliated members: ₹250 guest charge plus ₹250 per day, per person.

## Table tennis

Table tennis is available at the club. Contact the club for current access arrangements and charges.

## Gym

The club has a gym. Contact the club for access arrangements, timings, and charges.

## Locker room

The club has a locker room. Contact the team about access and locker arrangements.

## Sports lounge

The club has a sports lounge for time between games and socialising.

## Dining

The club menu includes breakfast, soups, vegetarian and non-vegetarian starters and main courses, Indian breads, rice, biryani, sides, beverages, and desserts. Members pay menu rates. Member guests and affiliated members pay menu rates plus ₹250 guest charge per person.

## Source and enquiries

All published prices above are in INR and come from the tariff card dated 2 September 2026. Confirm current rates and availability with the club; no opening hours or unlisted charges are implied.
