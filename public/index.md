## Chambers

Published rates from the club's tariff card dated 2 September 2026, in INR:

- Single occupancy: ₹3,000 per day.
- Double occupancy: ₹3,000 per day.
- Weekly: ₹16,000, minimum 7 days.
- Monthly: ₹46,000, minimum 28 days.
- Extra person: ₹1,500 per person, per bed.
- Member guests and affiliated members: tariff plus ₹250 guest charge per person.

Contact the club to confirm current tariffs, availability, and visiting arrangements. The website does not provide online reservation confirmation or payment processing.

## Club life

The eight facilities are tennis, badminton, billiards, table tennis, gym, locker room, sports lounge, and dining. Chambers are presented separately as accommodation.
