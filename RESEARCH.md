# Research and content provenance

Researched 30 September 2026. The user-supplied tariff PDF remains the authority for Yercaud Club rates and the 18-club affiliation list. The user confirmed all eight facilities: tennis, badminton, billiards, table tennis, gym, locker room, sports lounge and dining.

## Website references and decisions

- [Bamboo Club](https://bambooclub.in/): separates chambers, sports, dining, affiliations and contact. Adopted clear facility navigation and a dedicated affiliation page, not its text or imagery.
- [Coonoor Club](https://www.coonoorclub.com/): visible facilities navigation and photography-led presentation. Used as a structural reference; Yercaud’s supplied burgundy logo and own photographs remain the identity.
- [Kodaikanal Club](https://www.thekodaikanalclub.com/): distinct facilities and affiliate-club routes. Adopted a dedicated facilities page with individual anchor links.
- [Cochin Club](https://www.cochinclub.in/): combines club information with nearby places of interest. Added a short, clearly local Yercaud section, separate from facility imagery.

No memberships, equipment specifications, opening hours, dress codes or access rules have been copied from another club. No third-party club photographs are presented as Yercaud Club.

## Public mentions and verified information

- [Rama Varma Union Club affiliations](https://www.ramavarmaunionclub.com/affiliations): lists Yercaud Club, Jerina Nagar, Yercaud 636601, and yercaudclub@gmail.com. Address and email are used in the contact area. Its phone number differs from the supplied 2026 tariff card; that number is not used.
- [KSBA reciprocal affiliations](https://www.myksba.com/Affiliated.pdf): independently lists Yercaud Club and the same email. Its truncated phone number is not used.
- [Cauvery Peak history](https://cauverypeakestate.com/pages/history): mentions Yercaud Club in its account of local plantation leadership. Researched, but current office-holder claims and detailed historical narrative were not added because the material does not establish a complete club history.
- [Salem City Municipal Corporation places of interest](https://www.tnurbantree.tn.gov.in/salem/places-of-interest/): supports the geographical context of Yercaud in the Shevaroy Hills, Salem district. Linked from the new local-context section.
- Search results for Yercaud Library & Sports Club concern a separate institution (established 1875). Those images and history were excluded.
- Company aggregators report a 1901 incorporation date; the club’s own supplied materials state establishment in 1897. The website retains 1897 and does not conflate establishment and incorporation.
